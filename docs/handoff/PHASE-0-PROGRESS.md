## Device checks added

(populated as items land; consolidated into `docs/device-test/phase-0-checklist.md` at P0-19)

- (P0-03) A portrait photo from a camera that writes an EXIF rotation (for example a Samsung)
  opens upright in the editor, saves upright, and shares upright with a frame. The
  `ImageDecoder`-applies-orientation claim this task relies on is empirically confirmed in this
  sandbox (`BitmapDecodingImageDecoderExifTest`, real JPEG + `Orientation=6`, `@GraphicsMode(NATIVE)`)
  but only for a synthetic 4×6 bitmap re-encoded by this same JVM's `Bitmap.compress` — not a real
  camera JPEG's actual encoder quirks, ICC profile, or a HEIC/HEIF file, none of which this
  sandbox can produce or a device-free test can stand in for.
- (P0-04) Share a geotagged JPEG, HEIC and PNG to yourself (or another device) and check each in
  an EXIF viewer for no GPS — the HEIC case specifically exercises the `Unsupported` → re-encode
  path, which this sandbox can construct real bytes for but has no HEIC decoder to confirm against.
  Share a Samsung/Google motion photo and confirm the received file is a plain still JPEG, not the
  original's embedded-MP4-trailer format (this task's JPEG stripper stops at the first EOI
  specifically for this). Share a portrait photo with a Polaroid or stamp frame and confirm it
  arrives upright. Share a GIF or animated WebP with a frame selected and confirm the animation
  still plays on the receiving end rather than arriving as one static, framed image.
- (P0-05) Share a geotagged phone video (with "Remove location and camera data" on): the received
  file has no location (check in a metadata viewer, and separately confirm `MediaMetadataRetriever`
  on it directly if possible), it plays, and it is the right way up — this exercises real
  `MediaExtractor`/`MediaMuxer` behavior this sandbox has no device to confirm at all (no prior test
  in this repo drives them against a real file either). If the source is HEVC, VP8 or VP9, confirm
  the codec-appropriate container came out (MP4 for HEVC, WebM for VP8/VP9) and that it still plays
  in a normal video player. If the phone has an AV1-recorded video, confirm it fails closed with the
  "can't be shared without its location yet" message rather than silently going out unstripped —
  this task could not confirm `MediaMuxer`'s own AV1-in-MP4 support level and deliberately excluded
  it from `containerFor` rather than guess; a device check may reveal AV1 is actually safe to add.
- (P0-06) Select 5 photos (1 HEIC) and 1 video → Zip → share to Files → the archive opens with 6
  entries and no GPS in any of them. Confirm the HEIC entry's extension actually matches its real
  content (it will have been re-encoded to strip its location, per P0-04) and that a zip tool
  reports no compression (STORED-equivalent size) on the entries rather than the smaller size a
  normal DEFLATE archive would show.
