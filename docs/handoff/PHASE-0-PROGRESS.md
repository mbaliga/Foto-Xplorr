   Unlocking a folder or turning off "hide sensitive" will need a fresh manual "Index library" run

   `Videos`/`Screenshots`/`Favourites`/`Recent` smart albums and destinations can still show
   archived photos. Pre-existing behaviour, out of this item's scope to change — flagging in case
   it should be reconciled with `browsableAssets` in a later phase.
3. (P0-04, asked verbatim by the brief itself) Animated images and videos are never rendered with
   a frame or watermark — they are stripped and sent as-is (videos via P0-05, not yet done).
   Should a free-tier (non-Pro) sharer's animated or video share still carry the Foto Xplorr
   watermark some other way (e.g. a corner overlay baked into a video's first frame, or an
   animated stamp), or is "no mark on these two formats" acceptable indefinitely?
