A gallery for the photos and videos already on your phone. It opens fast, it does
not want an account, and it never uploads anything.

BROWSE
• Ten destinations: Pets, People, Identity, Screenshots, Photos, Videos, Audio,
  Favourites, Places and Protected.
• A timeline grouped by day or month, or ungrouped, with a scrubber.
• Search by filename, folder, media type, tag, date range or camera.
• Sort by newest, oldest, name or size, with an adjustable grid.
• A full-screen viewer with EXIF/XMP details, swipe, zoom, pan and slideshow.
• A full video/audio player with variable speed, subtitle and audio-track
  selection, gestures, resume position, and Picture-in-Picture.
• RAW (best effort), SVG, and animated GIF, WebP and AVIF.
• System, light and dark themes with several accent palettes; tablet and
  ChromeOS-friendly layouts.

EDIT
• A real photo editor: exposure, contrast, highlights/shadows, white balance,
  HSL, per-channel curves, crop, straighten, spot heal, vignette, sharpen,
  presets, and undo/redo with a saved edit history.
• Save a copy, Save As to a chosen format and destination, or replace the
  original — always behind Android's own confirmation for the last one.
• A video editor: trim, speed, rotate, mirror, mute, crop and colour filters,
  with a live preview and real export progress.
• Read and write EXIF/XMP: rating, creator, copyright, keywords and location.
• Convert a video to MP4 or an audio file to AAC, and edit MP3/MP4/FLAC tags.

ORGANISE, REVERSIBLY
• Favourites, an archive, a sensitive flag and your own tags, all persistent.
• On-device recognition (no network) for pets, people and identity documents.
• Collections that group media without moving the source files.
• Bulk selection, multi-share and bulk organisation, with visible progress.
• Copy to any folder through Android's storage access framework.
• A safe move that copies first and only then asks Android to trash the original.

DISCOVER
• Smart albums for favourites, recents, videos, screenshots, animated media,
  large files, possible duplicates, sensitive media, archive, trash and untagged.
• Location metadata extracted from your own files, on demand, on device.
• An offline coordinate map that downloads no tiles, plus an optional on-device
  compass view.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no network permission at all
  in this build.
• Your photos are never uploaded, and never used to train anything.
• Recognition, similarity and background playback all run locally.

Foto Xplorr is open source.
