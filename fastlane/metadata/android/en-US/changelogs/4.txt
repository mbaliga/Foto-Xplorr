A real photo and video editor: exposure, HSL, curves, crop, spot heal, presets
and undo history, with Save a copy, Save As, or replace the original.

Read and write EXIF/XMP metadata (rating, creator, copyright, keywords,
location). Convert video to MP4 and audio to AAC, and edit MP3/MP4/FLAC tags.

A new Audio destination: browse and play your device's audio with background
playback and lock-screen controls. Video playback gained variable speed,
subtitles, audio-track selection, gestures and Picture-in-Picture.

Long-running jobs (conversion, export, copy/move) now show progress and can be
cancelled, and the app survives rotation without losing your place.
