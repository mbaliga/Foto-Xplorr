# Privacy Policy — Foto Xplorr

> **This is the working copy, not the hosted one.** The URL Play Console points at
> is **https://asystemofcells.com/fotoxplorr/privacy**. Keep the two in sync by hand.

**Last updated: 29 August 2026**

Foto Xplorr is a photo gallery for Android (package `com.fotoxplorr.app`), made by
A System of Cells. It can see every photo on your phone, so this policy is specific
about what it does with them.

## The short version

Foto Xplorr has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. **Your
photos are never uploaded.** We operate no servers and receive nothing from the app.

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** No photos, no thumbnails, no metadata, no usage data.

## Your photos

The app reads the photos and videos already on your device in order to show them to
you. That is the whole reason it asks for media access.

- **Nothing is uploaded.** There is no cloud, no sync and no backup.
- **Nothing is used to train anything.**
- **Nothing is indexed remotely.** Everything the app knows about your library, the
  timeline, smart albums, duplicates, similarity, is computed on your device and
  stored on your device.
- If you use Android 14 or later, you can grant access to **selected photos only**,
  and the app works with that.

## Location in your photos

Some photos carry the coordinates of where they were taken, written by the camera
that took them. When you ask for it, the app reads that information **out of your
own files**, on your device, to place them on a map.

That map is **offline**. It downloads no tiles, contacts no map service, and the
app does not ask for or use your device's current location.

## Organising is reversible

Collections group your media without moving the underlying files. A move copies the
file first, and only then asks Android to put the original in the system trash. The
app tries hard never to be the reason a photo is gone.

## Optional AI features

Off unless you set them up. If you supply **your own API key** for a provider, then
the images you choose to run that feature on are sent to **that provider**, over
HTTPS, under **that provider's** privacy policy.

- This is a real transfer of your photos to a third party, and is worth stating
  plainly.
- It never happens unless you configured it and asked for it, image by image.
- We never see them. There is no proxy of ours in the middle.
- With the feature unused, the app makes no such requests at all.

## What is stored, and where

Your tags, favourites, archive state, sensitive flags, collections and settings are
stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling Foto Xplorr
deletes all of it. Your actual photos are untouched by an uninstall; they were never
the app's to begin with.

## Permissions, and why each exists

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` | To show you your own photos and videos. |
| `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED` | So you can grant access to selected photos only, if you prefer. |
| `INTERNET` | Only for the optional AI features you configured. |

## Children

Foto Xplorr is not directed at children and collects no personal information from
anyone, including children.
