A gallery for the photos already on your phone. It opens fast, it does not want an
account, and it never uploads anything.

BROWSE
• Photos, Albums, Discover and Library as four clear destinations.
• A timeline grouped by day or month, or ungrouped.
• Search by filename, folder, media type or your own tags.
• Sort by newest, oldest, name or size, with a grid from two to seven columns.
• A full-screen viewer with swipe, zoom, pan, metadata and a slideshow.
• Video playback in the app.
• SVG and animated GIF, WebP and AVIF.
• System, light and dark themes with several accent palettes.

ORGANISE, REVERSIBLY
• Favourites, an archive, a sensitive flag and your own tags, all persistent.
• Collections that group media without moving the source files.
• Device folders and virtual collections shown separately, so you always know
  which is which.
• Bulk selection, multi-share and bulk organisation.
• Copy to any folder through Android's storage access framework.
• A safe move that copies first and only then asks Android to trash the original.

DISCOVER
• Smart albums for favourites, recents, videos, screenshots, animated media, large
  files, possible duplicates, sensitive media, archive, trash and untagged.
• Location metadata extracted from your own files, on demand, on device.
• An offline coordinate map that downloads no tiles and never asks for your
  current location.

QUIET BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.
• Your photos are never uploaded, and never used to train anything.
• Face grouping and similarity work locally if you use them.
• Optional AI features are bring-your-own-key and off unless you set them up.

Foto Xplorr is open source.
